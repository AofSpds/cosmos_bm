## 0. Executive finding

The evidence supports **selective transfer**, not wholesale “6G integration.” The strongest transferable ideas are:

1. normalized observability and evidence exposure;
2. measurable SLO/SLA design with explicit exception and recovery states;
3. structured intent and capability exposure;
4. AI prediction separated from deterministic optimization, policy enforcement, authorization, and cryptographic execution;
5. limited route diversity, sequential failover, and provider-conflict controls.

The evidence does **not** support:

- claiming that Cosmos Hub implements or complies with 6G standards;
- translating wireless QoS into a global finality or principal guarantee;
- treating network slicing as a blockchain product primitive;
- placing private keys, live transaction authorization, compensation decisions, or policy-limit mutation under AI control;
- making a Hub-owned proprietary AI router the default architecture;
- default redundant or hedged asset execution before duplicate-suppression and recovery economics are proven.

**Working conclusion:** `B. SELECTIVE FIT`.
