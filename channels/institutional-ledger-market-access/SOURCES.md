## Source-use rules

- Exact Git path, byte-preserved source, and central SHA-256 record control over memory summaries or chat inference.
- Preserve Owner wording exactly in the Owner-evidence file; do not reconstruct missing evidence.
- Separate current implementation, source-supported fact, proposal, inference, and unverified hypothesis.
- Do not add unsupported general claims about institutions, regulation, demand, revenue, interoperability, or implementation.
- Institution-specific needs, buyer/payer identity, WTP, product bundles, and pilot readiness remain Candidate until supported by evidence and the required decision path.
- Do not convert attestation evidence into a regulatory-compliance claim or service expectations into a commercial guarantee.
- Record new sources with path, SHA-256, byte size, status, authority class, and caution before relying on them.
