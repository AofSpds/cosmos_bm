## LGEC-01 — responsibility and legal boundary

- Who is the quote issuer, execution-plan builder, policy approver, signer, evidence collector, breach adjudicator, recovery operator, compensation payer, and contracting party?
- Which events are provider-controllable, user-caused, external, exempt, or unresolved?
- Does any proposed Gateway, Router, Hub, CosmWasm, or wallet role create custody, agency, discretion, or fiduciary-like exposure?
- Which conclusions require external counsel, for which jurisdictions, and on what exact facts?

Minimum closure evidence: actor-by-state responsibility matrix, contract-party and invoice map, exact control boundary, jurisdiction assumptions, and a `COUNSEL REQUIRED` register.

## LGEC-02 — governance authority

- Who holds admin, pause, upgrade, revocation, and dispute authority?
- What are the least-privilege controls, multi-party approvals, incident escalation, and rollback conditions?
- How are Registry conflicts and provider eligibility disputes resolved without an opaque or monopolistic arbiter?

Minimum closure evidence: authority map, key/control policy, pause and upgrade procedure, revocation rules, dispute route, and audit trail.

## LGEC-03 — economics, reserve, and exposure

- What exact amount, per-event exposure, aggregate exposure, reserve/bond, and payout ceiling would apply?
- Who bears recovery, support, incident, and partial-leg accounting costs?
- Is net value still positive after these costs and locked-capital cost?
- What willingness-to-pay evidence exists, and how is protocol share distinguished from operator revenue?
- How are announced, contracted, accrued, received, ATOM-purchased, and Community-Pool-deposited amounts separately proven?

Minimum closure evidence: bounded exposure model, reserve/bond assumptions, unit-economics model, WTP evidence, and received-revenue attribution method.

## LGEC-04 — claim register

- Which statement is permitted at C0, C1, or C2 for each source and document?
- Which future evidence would be required for C3 or C4?
- How are guarantee, insurance, compliance, safety, and finality implications prevented in proposal, partner, governance, and marketing text?

Minimum closure evidence: claim-to-source crosswalk, maturity/status label, prohibited wording, approval workflow, and stale-claim review rule.

## LGEC-05 — adversarial findings

- Can provider ranking create discrimination, lock-in, or self-reinforcing concentration?
- Can a `non-custodial` design still create custody/agency risk in practice?
- Can a partial or duplicate execution create liability that exceeds fee revenue?
- Can institution data, policy, or trade secrets leak through public evidence records?
- Do any proposed claims become essential to the product only by exaggerating insurance, guarantee, compliance, or 6G status?

Minimum closure evidence: objection, source evidence, response, residual risk, minimum closure evidence, roadmap impact, and GO/HOLD/KILL for each finding.
