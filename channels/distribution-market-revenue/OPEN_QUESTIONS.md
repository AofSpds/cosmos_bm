# CH-01 Open Questions

All questions remain open Candidate-design questions. No Owner decision is inferred.

## Product and evidence

- What precise outcome will an issuer pay to buy repeatedly?
- Which baseline separates Campaign effect from market-wide price or activity growth?
- What evidence proves incremental attribution independently of issuer/provider self-report?
- Which holder, depth, retention, and usability measures establish outcome quality?
- Which wash, self-dealing, circular-flow, or sybil signals exclude activity?

## Payout and revenue

- When does a provider payout become final?
- Which gaming signals cause pre-payment holdback or post-payment clawback?
- What minimum Campaign/receipt/revenue state belongs on Hub/CosmWasm, and what stays off-chain?
- How are `CONTRACTED`, `ACCRUED`, and `RECEIVED` kept distinct end to end?
- What exact transaction evidence proves realized ATOM purchase or Community Pool attribution?
- How is externally paid revenue traced to the Revenue Vault without counting recycled subsidies or internal token circulation?

## MVP and gate

- What single customer value must a 3–6 month MVP validate?
- What price, contract, renewal, and 30/60/90-day retention evidence constitutes WTP?
- Do operating, independent measurement, and provider costs leave positive protocol contribution?
- Which CH-02, CH-06, and CH-07 inputs are required before `BM1-DIST-02`–`06` can be channel-sealed?

Owner action is not required for bounded design discussion. It becomes required for MVP selection, common-boundary change, text integration, pilot/implementation, live financial execution, or another separately controlled approval.
